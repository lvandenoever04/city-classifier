Beat the model

Hi! I've built a CNN (convolutional neural network) which has been trained to look at a section of a city's figure-ground diagram and guess the name of the city it belongs to. The thing is, it isn't very accurate, which left some room for me to create this guessing game. Guess which of 20 cities each patch of buildings comes from, then see what the neural network said! The model always sees only a 500 m × 500 m square, but you can toggle the difficulty to expand your vision. Building data © OpenStreetMap contributors, via BBBike extracts.
